AI Downscaling Pipeline

Global Model · 12 km
→
AI Downscaled · 1 km
1.Coarse NWP output from BharatFS (6 km) and AI global models — Pangu, FourCastNet, GraphCast.
2.Assimilation of Automatic Weather Stations, Automatic Rain Gauges and Doppler radar reflectivity.
3.Terrain-aware super-resolution produces a 1 km rainfall & temperature field per Panchayat.
4.Threshold rules convert the forecast into sowing, irrigation and spray advisories.
Demonstration dashboard. Forecast values shown here are procedurally generated mock data for interface design purposes — they are not real IMD output. A production build would connect to the IMD Gram Panchayat Level Weather Forecast API, Mausamgram and e-Gramswaraj.
Data sources referenced in the design: BharatFS (6 km) · IMD AWS/ARG network · Doppler Weather Radar · INSAT-3DR · AI models (Pangu, FourCastNet, GraphCast).
